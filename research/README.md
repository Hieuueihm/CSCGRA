# Research and analysis

This directory preserves reproducible experiments, analysis programs, tables,
and paper figures that previously lived in the mixed `analysis` workspace.

New experiment source may be committed here. Temporary environments, caches,
raw simulator output, and regenerated intermediate data belong under `work`
or `logs`.

Research results do not become active RTL until the corresponding source
change passes the repository sign-off gates and is listed as retained in
`docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md`. In particular, the QR material is a robustness study
only; the current solver remains LDLT.
