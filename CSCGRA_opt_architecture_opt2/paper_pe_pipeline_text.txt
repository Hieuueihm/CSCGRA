The optimized PE fabric keeps the two 4x4 clusters connected by a combinational column connection, while assigning clearer work to the four PE rows. Row0 and row1 perform MAC and accumulation for correlation, Gram, RHS, and matrix-vector products. Row2 contains the coefficient-update and pruning logic: it reuses a saturated add-shift primitive for IHT/GP updates, and its prune unit evaluates an 8-lane vector block to generate a support keep-mask. Row3 centralizes the 8-bank writeback path; during prune, it writes all valid lanes in the block in one step, preserving selected coefficients and writing zero to rejected lanes. The prune FSM advances by eight vector entries per step, so the context format and golden fixed-point flow are unchanged while per-element zero-out cycles are removed. The LS service is also described as a four-stage path: row0/row1 accumulate Gram/RHS terms, row2 performs Gaussian row/RHS update, and row3 writes the refined coefficients. Golden-checked simulation shows that block-8 pruning preserves all final vectors: noisy24 K=8 passes 8/8 algorithms, full k-sweep passes 348/348 checks, and M=128,N=256,K=8 passes 45/45 checks. For M=64,N=256,K=8, the block-8 prune reduces IHT from 75,843 to 72,259 cycles and HTP from 114,019 to 110,435 cycles; in the noisy24 representative final runs, IHT reduces from 149,757 to 142,589 cycles and HTP from 450,641 to 436,305 cycles.
